Who we are

A second pair of eyes for your job search

ResumeFit was built by a small team who spent too many hours guessing why applications went unanswered. We turned that frustration into a tool: a plain-language resume checker that compares your document against a real job description and tells you, in clear terms, what to change.

No jargon, no false promises of a guaranteed interview — just a clear, honest read on how your resume matches the role, so you can apply with confidence.

What guides our work
