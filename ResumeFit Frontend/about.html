About ResumeFit

We help good resumes get read

Most resumes aren't rejected because the person isn't qualified — they're rejected because the resume doesn't speak the same language as the job description. We built ResumeFit to close that gap.

Our story

Built from a frustrating job search

ResumeFit started as a small side project after one of our team members applied to over sixty roles and heard back from almost none of them. After reformatting the same resume to match each job description more closely, the responses started coming in. The content hadn't changed — only the wording and structure had.

That experiment became the basis for ResumeFit: a tool that compares a resume against a job description, highlights the overlap, and explains in plain terms what to adjust.

🎯

Our mission

To give every job seeker the same advantage that comes naturally to recruiters and hiring managers: a clear view of how a resume reads against the role it's applied to, regardless of background or budget.

Why it matters

How resume optimization helps job seekers

Optimization isn't about tricking software — it's about making sure your real experience is described in a way that both software and people can quickly recognize as relevant.

🔍

Gets past automated screening

Many companies use applicant tracking systems to scan resumes for relevant terms before a person ever sees them. Matching the language of the job description helps your resume clear that first filter.

⏱️

Saves recruiters time

A resume that mirrors the job posting's structure and terms is faster for a recruiter to scan, which means your strongest points are more likely to be noticed in the first few seconds.

📈

Builds application confidence

Knowing exactly which skills are missing — or already strong — helps you decide which roles are worth applying to and which need a quick edit first.

What we stand for

The principles behind ResumeFit
