Pricing questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move between Free, Pro, and Career at any time, and changes take effect from your next billing date.

Is there a free trial for Pro or Career?

The Free plan lets you try the core resume check at no cost. Reach out through the Contact Us page if you'd like to discuss a trial of Pro or Career.

Do unused checks roll over to the next month?

No. Free plan checks reset each month and do not carry over, while Pro and Career include unlimited checks.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime from your account settings. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.
