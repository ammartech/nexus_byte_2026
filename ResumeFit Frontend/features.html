Services & Features

Everything you need to tailor a resume, in one place

ResumeFit combines resume analysis, keyword matching, ATS-friendly suggestions, and skill improvement recommendations into a single, straightforward workflow.

Core features

What ResumeFit checks for you

📋

Resume analysis

ResumeFit reads through the structure of your resume — sections, headings, and formatting — and flags anything that could make it harder for an applicant tracking system or a recruiter to scan quickly.

🔑

Keyword matching

Paste in a job description and ResumeFit compares it to your resume, showing which important skills and terms already appear and which ones are missing.

🤖

ATS-friendly suggestions

Get specific, plain-language suggestions for making your resume easier for applicant tracking systems to parse correctly, without resorting to keyword stuffing.

📈

Skill improvement recommendations

Beyond wording, ResumeFit points out skill areas worth strengthening for the roles you're targeting, based on what consistently appears in similar job descriptions.

How it works

From upload to action plan in three steps

Step 1

Upload your resume

Add your resume as a PDF or Word document. Your file is processed for the check and is not shared with third parties.

Step 2

Paste the job description

Copy in the text of the role you're applying for so ResumeFit can compare your resume's language against it.

Step 3

Review your match report

See your match score, the keywords found and missing, and a list of specific suggestions to apply before you submit your application.
